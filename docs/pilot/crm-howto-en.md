# WehdatHub clients (CRM) guide for brokers (English)

Send this after the welcome message (`docs/pilot/broker-howto-en.md`) and the website guide (`docs/pilot/web-howto-en.md`). Arabic version: `docs/pilot/crm-howto-ar.md`.

---

👤 *Your clients in WehdatHub*

*➕ Saving a client*
• *Forward* the client's WhatsApp message to the bot, or share their *contact card*: tap *➕ Save as client*.
• Type *lead Sara 0101 234 5678 wants a 3-bed in New Cairo*, or say it in a voice note.
• Many clients at once: website → *Clients* → *Import* (CSV or Excel, template on the page). A Facebook/Instagram Leads Center download loads as it is.
• A number that is already saved opens the existing client instead of a copy. A colleague's client: you are told and your manager is informed.

*📞 Who is calling?*
Send the bare number (or forward the contact card). If it's your client you get their card; if not, you can save them.

*🗂️ The client card*
Each client has a reference like *L-0042*. Send *L-0042* any time to open the card.
• *📞 Called*: logs the call.
• *📅 Follow-up*: sets the next step (tomorrow, in 2 days, a date).
• *🔄 Stage*: new → contacted → interested → viewing → negotiation → won / lost.
• *↩️ Undo* appears after every change for a short time.
Type *my leads* for your list, *follow-ups* for what's due.

*📅 Follow-ups and viewings*
At the time you set, the bot reminds you (several due at once come in one message). Tap *✅ Done* or *📅 Postpone*. A viewing reminder includes the unit and its place. A new client nobody has contacted after 24 hours gets one nudge.

*🎙️ One voice note does it all*
"Called Ahmed, he wants to view VIL-08 on Friday at 5." The bot shows what it understood (stage, viewing time, unit, note). Tap *✅ Confirm* to save it all, *✏️ Edit* to correct, or *↩️ Undo* afterwards. Nothing is saved before you confirm.

*🔔 Requests and matches*
• *request L-0042 3 bedrooms New Cairo under 9M* saves what the client wants. When a matching unit is added later, you get an alert.
• A search with no results offers *🔔 Save as request*.
• A unit page shows *🔥 N clients want this unit*, with a *Send* button for each.
• *Send to client* opens WhatsApp with the unit's link ready. If the client opens your links 3 times in 2 days you get a *🔥 hot client* alert.

*🔔 Notifications*
• Website → *⚙️ Settings*: for each kind of alert choose *WhatsApp* or *Website*, and set quiet hours (default 22:00–08:00; alerts wait until morning).
• Turn on website notifications on your phone: *⚙️ Settings* → *🔔 Turn on here* → allow.
• *iPhone:* open the site in *Safari* → *Share* → *Add to Home Screen*, then open WehdatHub from the new icon and turn notifications on there (iOS 16.4 or later).
• Everything also waits in the website inbox (🔔).

*💬 How WhatsApp credits work*
Every message the bot sends you costs the agency 1 credit from its monthly balance. Roughly: a search 1, a client copy 1, a flyer 1, adding a unit about 5, saving a client 2. *Free:* everything on the website, website notifications and read ticks. See your use on the website under *📊 My usage* (managers see the whole agency).
• At 80% you get a hint, at 100% alerts move to the website (replies continue).
• At the ceiling the bot sends one notice a day and then only marks messages as read, until next month or until your manager raises the balance.

Fine on WhatsApp: one quick search, a quick update, one client copy, forwarding a client, a voice note. *Tip:* send the details in one message, not five.

*🤝 Deals and commission*
• WhatsApp: *deal L-0042 VIL-08 8.5M commission 2%* (add *split 60/40* to share with the unit's broker, or *split 60/40 with Mona*). Review, then *✅ Confirm*.
• Website: the client's page → *🤝 Record deal*, or *🤝 Deals*.
• Confirming marks the client *won* and your unit *sold* together. You see your own share; managers see the whole commission and can correct it.

*👀 Who sees what*
• Units: the whole agency.
• Clients: you see the clients assigned to you; managers see all and assign them.
• Deals: you see the deals you're part of and only your share.
• Commission never appears in anything sent to a client.

*🗑️ A client asks to be deleted*
Tell your manager. On the client's page they can *📦 Export* the client's data (for a "what do you hold on me" request) or *🗑️ Erase* the client permanently. Clients with no activity for 18 months are erased automatically.

*✅ Do's and ❌ don'ts with client data*
✅ Save clients in WehdatHub, not in screenshots or personal notes.
✅ Write only what's needed for the deal.
❌ Don't forward a client's number or details to people outside the agency.
❌ Don't share your sign-in link; each link works once, for you.
❌ Don't put the client's ID, salary or personal remarks in notes.
