# WehdatHub website guide for brokers (short, English)

Send this to English-speaking brokers with the welcome message (`docs/pilot/broker-howto-en.md`). Arabic version: `docs/pilot/web-howto-ar.md`.

---

🌐 *WehdatHub website*: the same inventory as on WhatsApp, on a bigger screen.

*🔐 Signing in*
Send *login* to the WehdatHub number on WhatsApp and you get a link. Open it and tap *Sign in*. You stay signed in for 30 days on that device.
• The link works once and for 15 minutes. Don't forward it.
• On your phone: in the browser menu choose *Add to Home screen* so it works like an app.
• *English / عربي* at the top switches the language of every page.

*🔍 Search*
Type your request as on WhatsApp ("3-bedroom apartment in New Cairo under 9M"), or use *🎛️ Refine* and pick filters (place, price, bedrooms, delivery, payment, features…).
• The same results in the same order as on WhatsApp, all on one page.
• Every WhatsApp search ends with a *Refine and see all results* link that opens the same search here.
• Tap *⭐ Save search* and come back later: it tells you how many units are *new*.

*➕ Adding and editing*
• *➕ Add*: fill in the fields, or paste the description and tap *✨ Fill from text*, or tap *🎙️ Record voice* and speak.
• Tap *📋 Review* to see what is missing before saving, then *✅ Save*.
• Photos: up to 15 per unit.
• Edit from the unit page with *✏️ Edit*. If someone edits the same unit at the same time, the site asks you to reopen the edit (no change is lost).

*📥 Importing*
• *Excel sheet*: upload the sheet (or the export from the bot). You see what is new and what changed (from → to) before anything is saved, and choose what to apply.
• *Group posts*: paste the posts (two empty lines between posts) or upload a WhatsApp *chat export*. Units are read and duplicates are flagged.
• *Bulk edit*: on the *Units* page tick units and choose: still available, sold, archive, or change the price by a percentage.

*👤 Clients and deals*
• *Clients*: add one, import a sheet, see each client's requests, matches, timeline and next follow-up.
• *Notifications*: reminders and alerts, with *Done* and *Postpone* on follow-ups.
• *🤝 Deals*: record a deal from the client's page; you see your own share of the commission.

*🔗 Link for the client*
The *Client copy* on WhatsApp includes a link to the unit's page (photos and details only, nothing internal). On the website: unit page → *New link*.
👀 You get a WhatsApp alert when the client opens the link.

Any problem? Send *help* to the bot or talk to your agency manager.
